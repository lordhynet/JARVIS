# Typed protected check-ins

ForgeCheckInPayload is the versioned plaintext body of the existing encrypted ForgeCheckIns row. It records bounded owner reports or existing AgentSourceRef kind/ID/version references to canonical data, not another scoring, evidence, coaching, finance or history store. Canonical-reference observations may not embed copied values/answers/scores/units. They remain unresolved references; structural validation is not source verification, professional permission, owner authorization or competence evidence. Future commands must resolve installed division/metric rules and every source's owner/current version/status in the existing transaction.

Null observations mean not supplied; an empty list explicitly means none. Unknown values/times remain null; zero and false remain known owner reports. Numeric values need explicit units; numeric/text/boolean forms are mutually exclusive. Owner reports cannot attach a canonical source to impersonate verified data. Consent/version/key/count/text/provenance bounds are checked; notes trimmed, typed observations defensively copied/sorted and duplicate metrics refused. Domain key grammar reuses DomainPolicy.IsValidKey without changing registry behavior. No scores, coverage, diagnoses, financial ledger or immigration conclusions are generated. Paused owner editing remains valid, with no automatic dispatch.

Payloads are private request-scoped plaintext solely for protection or explicit authorized export; use existing padded ForgeProtectedJson and scoped typed decoding. Its reader now rejects unknown members throughout the object graph, including nested existing AgentSourceRef, rather than silently dropping future/private fields. Known older unpadded JSON remains readable without rewrite. Managed-memory limits remain those in ADR0032; no logs/caches/events/tasks/AI/display projection. Retry comparison is normalized canonical request bytes plus the existing MAC/context, never list/record equality.

No table/migration, public contract/route, handler/key DI, data collection, planner or activation is added. E1 authorized decrypted export of every retained revision, D1 operational database-plus-retained-key recovery, owner step-up and retention still gate real entry. This prepares the future exporter/commands, not a finished entry feature. Existing interview programme and canonical diagnostics remain unchanged.
