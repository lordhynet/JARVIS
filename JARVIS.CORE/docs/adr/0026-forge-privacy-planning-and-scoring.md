# Forge privacy, planning and scoring

Status: proposed (F0).

**Privacy.**
- Until an encrypted evidence vault exists (F10), Forge stores **metadata only** for evidence, immigration, finance and relationships: titles, dates, issuer, where the original is kept, consent and verification status. It stores no documents.
- WEALTH check-ins hold owner-entered totals, never account identifiers.
- Passport, immigration, debt and relationship content is never placed in generic agent prompts or context selection.
- The read-only display snapshot (screensaver, watch, Android "Today") carries only programme, phase, calendar progress and the last approved score with its date. It supports a privacy mode, and **never shows balances, debts, immigration status or relationship data on an unattended screen.**
- New tables join privacy export and erasure. The audit stores metadata, not sensitive content.

**Planning.** The weekly planner is **deterministic code**, not a model.
- **Hard constraints:** committed work, a 7–9 hour sleep window, quiet hours, legal work restrictions, recovery, exam and interview deadlines, and the owner's weekly capacity.
- **Priority order:** employment and immigration deadlines, then technical foundations, then maintenance and enrichment.
- **Time accounting:** one activity is never counted twice in total time, though it may carry several goal tags. Commute audio is tracked separately and never counts as independent coding. A buffer is reserved.
- **Interview rules:** an interview within 7 days increases targeted practice. Within 24 hours, review and sleep come first and optional tasks are deferred.
- **Replanning:** at most daily, or on a significant change.
- **Missed tasks** are moved or dropped, never piled up, and **the plan shows what was dropped and why.**
- **Calendar writes** need owner approval each time.
- A model may only *explain* a plan.

**Scoring.** The weekly score uses the addendum §19 weights, applied only to divisions that have evidence.
- **Coverage** (the share of weight with evidence) is always shown.
- **Missing data is "unknown"**, never 0 or 100.
- Manual overrides are audited.
- There are no shame scores, personality diagnoses, health diagnoses or relationship-compatibility scores.

**External actions.** Forge never messages anyone, posts publicly, books exams, submits applications, applies for or moves money, or marks a business opportunity validated on an agent's opinion. Every stage change in the opportunity pipeline needs explicit owner approval and a lawful-activity confirmation.
